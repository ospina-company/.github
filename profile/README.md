# Ospina Company

We help companies choose, buy, and run their technology well.

Ospina works as an embedded extension of a client's team on the problems that
sit between a business decision and a technical one: what to buy, from whom, on
what terms, and whether the thing you already bought is doing what you were
told it would. That covers sourcing and vendor selection, running a real RFP,
managing the rollout, and staying involved after go-live, when most vendors
have moved on to the next sale.

We also build software. Internal tools, client platforms, data and reporting
work, and the automation that makes an operation cheaper to run than it was
last quarter.

## How we work

Every engagement starts with the same question: what decision are you actually
trying to make? Most technology spend goes wrong long before implementation, in
a requirements document written to match a vendor's brochure.

We stay vendor-neutral. Clients contract directly with the suppliers they
select. We are not an OEM or a reseller working a quota, and we are not a systems
integrator angling for the next phase of work.

How an engagement is structured depends on the problem. Most are consulting or
project-based delivery on ordinary commercial terms. Where a client runs
large, ongoing vendor spend, an agent-of-record arrangement can make more
sense, and we will tell you which one fits your situation before you ask.
